Visualizing Training Cheatsheet

Recording history and plotting loss/accuracy curves. From Lesson 9.

Record during training

history = {"train_loss": [], "train_acc": [], "test_acc": []}
# each epoch:
history["train_loss"].append(loss)
history["train_acc"].append(acc)
history["test_acc"].append(test_acc)   # evaluate X_test every epoch too

Plot

fig, axes = plt.subplots(1, 2, figsize=(10, 4))
axes[0].plot(history["train_loss"]); axes[0].set_title("Training Loss")
axes[1].plot(history["train_acc"], label="train")
axes[1].plot(history["test_acc"], label="test")
axes[1].set_title("Accuracy"); axes[1].legend()
plt.savefig(Path(__file__).parent / "training_curves.png")

Reading the shapes

PatternMeaning
Loss: smooth drop, then flatworking as intended
Loss: barely moveslearning rate too low / already converged
Loss: spiky, jumps aroundlearning rate too high
Accuracy: train ≫ testoverfitting
Accuracy: train ≈ test, both lowunderfitting — more capacity or epochs
